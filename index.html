Course guide · Ages 15-18 · No coding needed

Build with AI

AI tools can write a lot of the code for an app now. What they can't do is decide what's worth building, or check that it really works for the people who'll use it. This guide teaches you to do both, using free tools, one small project at a time.

By the end of the course you can

  • Turn an unclear need into a short, testable plan.
  • Hand clear, small jobs to AI helpers, and check what comes back.
  • Test, fix and safely share a working tool.
  • Build a real tool for a real person, and hand it over.

What you need

  • A free Google account (Docs, Sheets and Forms).
  • A free AI chat assistant that your school has approved. Check each tool's age rules first.
  • Pen and paper for planning.
For teachers

Every chapter ends with a teacher box (timing, common sticking points, how to assess) and an APA reference list. The stories come from one real project, a live-class tool built with AI helpers, and use no student names or data.
